INT. ABANDONED TEMPLE - NIGHT

Heavy rain echoes outside. A lone archaeologist, ARJUN (32), enters the ruined temple holding a flickering lantern. Dust floats through the air as ancient carvings glow faintly on the walls.

Arjun slowly walks toward a massive stone door engraved with celestial symbols. He places his hand on the center seal.

The ground begins to tremble.

A hidden mechanism activates.

The stone door opens halfway, revealing a dark chamber illuminated by blue fire torches.

Inside the chamber sits an ancient metallic throne surrounded by floating fragments of glowing scripture.

Arjun steps forward carefully.

Suddenly, shadow-like humanoid guardians emerge from the darkness.

The lantern light flickers violently.

One guardian lunges toward Arjun.

CUT TO BLACK.
